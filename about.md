I am a Backend Engineer at Xflow, where I build core finance infrastructure: real-time payment and bank management systems that run with zero downtime, and the ML that works alongside them.

I specialize in distributed, time-critical systems, with a particular focus on the high-stakes environment of payment processing where durability and data consistency are paramount. At Xflow, I developed production-grade financial systems and implemented FX market forecasting under strict resource constraints. My background also includes experience at Couchbase, where I worked on the internals of the Magma storage engine and highly distributed cloud storage.

My areas of interest include distributed systems, storage engines, stream processing, information retrieval, and ML for financial systems.

Alongside industry work, I enjoy research. I’ve published at IEEE ICCAR 2025 (SenseNav: zero-shot object navigation using vision-language models), in Data in Brief (security datasets for LLM-based attack detection, from my time at PES University’s ISFCR lab), and co-authored a paper at ICTIS (Springer LNNS) on a knowledge-graph-enhanced RAG system for narrative analysis of crime films.

I love contributing to open source. I have a merged PR in HuggingFace Transformers that speeds up image processors for vision-language models, and I built LLMAO, a framework for updating LLM knowledge in real time.

Community matters a lot to me. I volunteer with Hasgeek, working behind the scenes to make events happen: the coordination and logistics that let a room full of engineers focus on the talks. At PES University, I was a core member of Google Developer Student Club (GDSC), Vega Racing Electric, The Innovation Lab and several other clubs.

If any of this resonates with you, whether it’s systems, research, or community, do not hesitate to reach out.
